## 1. How to use this

You do not have one demo. You have **one machine and six routes through it.** Pick the route from who is standing in front of you, not from what you feel like showing.

The whole point of the product is that *suggested*, *saved*, *prepared*, *sent* and *confirmed* are different states. Every scenario below exists to make one of those boundaries visible. If a scenario doesn't land a boundary, cut it.

**Golden rule:** press **Start over** before every new visitor. A stale Trip Plan from the last person makes you look careless about exactly the thing you claim to be careful about.

## 3. The scenarios

### S0 — The hook (30 seconds, standing)

**Path:** Don't touch the screen. Point at the status bar.

**Say:** *"That line never lies. Right now it says nothing saved, nothing booked, nothing sent. It says that because it's true, and it keeps saying it until the traveller does something deliberate."*

**Proves:** We are not a booking engine wearing a chat interface.

**Then:** *"Want to see what happens when someone actually answers?"* → S1 or S2.

---

### S1 — Two operators, one traveller ⭐ *the differentiator*

**Path:** `Two of us` → `Not decided yet` → `A small group is fine` → **`Day trips`** → `That's everything`

**On screen:**
> **2 operators** publish something that fits: Seville Tours Co. and Áloratur. Each confirms and books their own.

Three cards follow — Granada & the Alhambra, Córdoba (Seville Tours Co.), Caminito del Rey (Áloratur) — each carrying:
> Source: aloratur.com · read on 2026-09-18 · shown with the operator's permission

**Say:** *"Two real operators. Both gave written permission. Every price on this screen is the price they published, and the screen tells you where we read it and when. We don't rank by commercial interest — when two fit, we say two fit."*

**Proves:** Neutrality is a visible mechanism, not a promise.

**Trap:** Do **not** say "partnered" or "onboard". Say *"agreed to be shown"*. Permission to display ≠ partnership.

> **Variant:** `Local food` also produces two operators (Private Seville Tapas Tour €65 · Caminito del Rey, Álora and tapas €65). Useful when someone says *"day trips are easy, what about in-city?"*

---

### S2 — The honest no-match ⭐ *the operator-winner*

**Path:** `Two of us` → `Not decided yet` → `A small group is fine` → **`A relaxed pace`** → `That's everything`

**On screen:**
> Nothing in these catalogues matches those interests directly. Here is what the operators publish — a private request may suit you better.

**Say:** *"Pace isn't a product. No operator publishes 'relaxed' as a SKU — it's a judgement they apply on the day. So we don't pretend to match it. We say so, and we offer to ask a human instead."*

**Proves:** The system knows the edge of its own knowledge. This is the moment operators stop treating you as a threat.

**Why it works:** Every AI product at that fair will demo its best case. You are demoing your worst case on purpose. Nobody else will.

---

### S3 — The private request (the boundary)

**Path:** `Two of us` → `I have specific dates` → `A day either way` → **`Private, if available`** → `Local food` → `That's everything` → **`Prepare a private request`**

**On screen, before you even press anything:**
> Private is a preference, not a product. An operator has to confirm it.

Then, in the Trip Plan:
> Status — **Not sent yet**
> **Nothing has been sent to an operator.**

**Say:** *"Choosing 'private' did not create anything. It took a second, deliberate action to even prepare a request — and preparing still isn't sending. The traveller holds it. The operator hasn't seen it."*

**Proves:** Intent ≠ commitment. No phantom demand, no wasted operator time.

**Trap:** Someone will ask *"so how does it get sent?"* The honest answer is: *"That's the next build. Today the handoff is deliberate and manual because getting the boundary right mattered more than getting the plumbing fast."*

---

### S4 — Her own words

**Path:** From S3's end state, type into the free-text box:

> `My mother uses a walking stick and we cannot manage stairs.`

**On screen:**
> Kept in your own words. The operator reads it exactly as you wrote it.

And in the Journey Brief, under **In your own words**, the sentence appears in quotation marks — unparsed, uncategorised, unsummarised.

**Say:** *"We didn't turn that into `accessibility: true`. The moment you tokenise it, you've decided what matters — and you'll be wrong about somebody's mother. The operator gets the sentence."*

**Proves:** Structured context without lossy abstraction. Lands hardest with guides and small operators, who have all been burned by a booking note that got truncated.

---

### S5 — The questions we refuse to answer ⭐ *the liability shield*

**Path:** From S3/S4, tap **`Accessibility needs`** and **`Somewhere to stay`** under *"Anything you would like the operator to advise on?"*

**On screen:**
> We pass the question on. We do not arrange or price any of these.

And in the handover panel:
> We pass the question on. We do not arrange, price or combine these into a package — the operator answers with what they can offer.

**Say:** *"The moment you combine accommodation and a tour and sell them together, you're a package organiser under EU Directive 2015/2302 and you carry insolvency and performance liability. We don't want it and our operators don't want us to have it. So these are questions, not products."*

**Proves:** You understand the regulatory line and designed to it deliberately, not accidentally.

**Who this wins:** DMCs, incoming agencies, anyone with a compliance function. This is the single most credible thing on the stand for that audience.

---

### S6 — The full arc (2–3 minutes, seated)

**Path:** Run S3 → S4 → S5, then add a saved experience with **`Add to Trip Plan`**, then press **`See what an operator would receive`**.

**On screen — the handover panel, verbatim:**
> **What an operator would receive**
> Nothing has been sent. No operator has seen this yet.
>
> Travellers · Two adults
> Dates · Specific dates
> Experiences you saved · Private Seville Tapas Tour · Seville Tours Co. · €65 per person · nothing booked
> Asked us to arrange · A private Seville experience · not sent
> Interests · Local food
> In your own words · *"My mother uses a walking stick and we cannot manage stairs."*
>
> **Still for the operator to decide** — availability on the requested dates · whether a private format is possible · final price and what it includes · meeting point and timings
>
> **Questions for the operator** — Accessibility needs · Somewhere to stay

**Say:** *"That's the product. Not the chat — the artefact. An operator opens this and knows in ten seconds who's asking, what they've already engaged with, what's still theirs to decide, and what the traveller actually said. And the top line still reads 'nothing has been sent'."*

**Proves:** The whole thesis in one screen. **This is your closing frame — don't rush to it, and don't skip it.**

## 4. Exact labels (verified — don't fumble these)

| Question | Options, in screen order |
|---|---|
| Who is travelling to Seville? | Just me · **Two of us** · Family with children · Group of five or more |
| When are you thinking of coming? | I have specific dates · A rough window · **Not decided yet** |
| How fixed are those dates? *(skipped if "Not decided yet")* | Fixed · **A day either way** · Quite flexible |
| How do you prefer to travel? | A small group is fine · **Private, if available** · Built around us |
| What pulls you? Choose as many as you like. | History · Local food · Architecture · Nature and landscape · **Day trips** · Local life · **A relaxed pace** → then **That's everything** |
| Anything you would like the operator to advise on? *(optional)* | Somewhere to stay · Getting around · Where to eat · Accessibility needs |

**The date question hides a detail worth pointing out:** answer *"Not decided yet"* and the flexibility question never appears. *"We don't ask questions we can't use the answer to."* Small, but people notice it.

## 5. Failure drills

Rehearse these. Something will go wrong on day two.

| If… | Do this | Say this |
|---|---|---|
| Wi-Fi dies | Nothing. The route reads a local manifest and fetches nothing at runtime. | *"Still working. It doesn't need the internet — that's deliberate."* |
| A **Check dates** link opens | It opens in a new tab; close it. State survives. | *"That's the operator's own booking system. We hand off, we don't hold it."* |
| Screen state is confusing | **Start over**, redo in 20 seconds. | *"Let me show you cleanly."* |
| Laptop dies | Fall back to S0 + the leave-behind card. | — |
| Asked for a language other than English | Don't switch. Narrate in Spanish over the English screen. | *"The product runs in four languages. This particular playback is English-only — I'd rather show you something real than something half-translated."* |
| Asked about traction | Answer straight. | *"A working, operator-grounded demonstration and no validated market traction. That's why we're here."* |

## 6. What not to click

- Don't open **Full screen** for a 30-second visitor — the exit is one more thing to fumble.
- Don't click **Remove** unless you're demonstrating removal; focus handling after removal is a known rough edge (F4).
- Don't demo on the phone at 400px width if you can avoid it — the scroll-to-top button clips text (F5).
- Don't navigate away from `/playback`. The rest of the site is not part of this story.

---

## 7. Nightly (all three evenings)

Five minutes, both of you, before you leave the hall:

1. Which scenario did people respond to most? Ratio, not vibes.
2. Did anyone say something from the **forbidden list**? What, to whom?
3. What question did we fail to answer well?
4. Which scenario got cut for time repeatedly — and should it be retired?
5. One thing to change tomorrow. Only one.
