# Operator Handoff Field Card — TIS 2026

**Status:** Evidence instrument. **Independent of the demo.** This card stays useful even if the handoff concept screens are never built.
**Version:** 1.0 — 2026-09-21
**Purpose:** collect, at the stand, the one number that decides whether operator-to-operator context handoff is a company, a premium feature, or a false lead.
**Discipline:** [interview guide](./operator-interview-guide-2026-09.md) §1 — never pitch, past facts only, ask for the artefact.
**Related:** [operator problem map](./operator-problem-map-2026-09.md) · [battle plan](./tis-2026-battle-plan.md) · [scope assessment](../architecture/tis-handoff-concept-scope-2026-09.md)

**Print:** A6, double-sided, one card per conversation. Bring 20. Bring a pen.

---

## 0. Before the card — the three-question router (memorised, not printed)

Ask these standing up, in under sixty seconds, to decide whether this person can give us evidence at all.

| # | EN | ES |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What part of the traveller journey do you handle? | ¿De qué parte del viaje te encargas tú? |
| 2 | Where does the actual traveller conversation happen? | ¿Dónde ocurre realmente la conversación con el viajero? |
| 3 | When the request falls outside what you can do, what happens next? | Cuando la solicitud se sale de lo que puedes hacer, ¿qué pasa después? |

**Continue** if Q3 produces a real story about passing work to another professional.
**Exit politely** otherwise. Hotels, DMOs, platforms and investors are welcome conversations — they are not this card.

---

## 1. Front of card — qualification

### Q1 · The gate

> **EN** — When did you last pass a traveller request to another operator?
>
> **ES** — ¿Cuándo fue la última vez que pasaste una solicitud de un viajero a otro operador?

### Q2 · The story (only if Q1 is recent)

> **EN** — What did you send, what did the other operator have to ask again, and who remained responsible to the traveller?
>
> **ES** — ¿Qué le mandaste, qué te tuvo que volver a preguntar y quién siguió dando la cara ante el viajero?

*Then shut up.* Let them narrate. Do not prompt with "context", "brief" or "handoff".

### Q3 · Frequency, value, friction — all three, in this order

> **EN** — Roughly how many similar requests did you pass during your last comparable season?
>
> **ES** — Aproximadamente, ¿cuántas solicitudes parecidas pasaste durante tu última temporada comparable?

> **EN** — Approximately what kind of booking value was involved?
>
> **ES** — ¿Qué valor de reserva estaba aproximadamente en juego?

> **EN** — How well does the way you do it today actually work? One to five.
>
> **ES** — ¿Cómo de bien funciona hoy la forma en que lo haces? Del uno al cinco.

**Do not disqualify on frequency alone.** Six handoffs worth €20,000 beat fifty worth nothing.

**Do not score anything in front of the operator.** Keep the live conversation to five plain questions — when did it last happen, how many similar cases last season, what value was involved, what went wrong or took work, is the current workaround good enough. The comparison and weighting happen afterwards, at the desk, in §5.

### Fixed probes (use freely, all four)

| EN | ES |
|---|---|
| Can you show me? | ¿Me lo puedes enseñar? |
| Was that typical or unusual? | ¿Eso fue normal o fue un caso raro? |
| How many similar cases last comparable season? | ¿Cuántos casos parecidos en tu última temporada comparable? |
| Roughly what booking value was involved? | Aproximadamente, ¿qué valor de reserva estaba en juego? |

## 3. "What do I get?" — the verbal answer

Asked the moment they understand the concept. Answer verbally. **Do not show a commission, referral-benefit or settlement screen — none exists.**

> **EN** — "Initially, you receive visible attribution for the introduction and preserve your relationship with the traveller. We want to learn whether that professional recognition and reciprocal flow matter before introducing commissions or financial settlement. Existing booking and commercial systems can continue handling the transaction."

> **ES** — "Al principio, queda visible que la introducción vino de ti y mantienes tu relación con el viajero. Queremos comprobar si ese reconocimiento profesional y el flujo recíproco tienen valor antes de introducir comisiones o liquidaciones. La reserva y el acuerdo comercial siguen en los sistemas que ya utilizáis."

Then immediately convert it back into a research question:

> **EN** — "Would attribution alone matter to you, or would you need a financial arrangement?"
>
> **ES** — "¿Te importaría que la introducción quedara atribuida, o necesitarías un acuerdo económico?"

**All six answers are evidence, none is a failure:** attribution is enough · reciprocal referrals matter · commission is required · customer-relationship control matters most · quality assurance matters most · nothing makes passing worthwhile.

## 4. Language discipline at the stand

**The rule is not a banned vocabulary. It is a ban on unsupported positive state claims.** *"Nothing has been booked"*, *"availability has not been checked"* and *"no request was sent"* are exactly the sentences we want — they use the same words, and they are true.

**Never say (positive claims nothing supports):**

> Booking confirmed · Available now · Request sent · Operator accepted · Price confirmed

**Always say:**

> No booking created · Availability not confirmed · No price quoted · No real operator contacted · Concept demonstration

**Substitutions that carry the boundary:**

- **prepares context for the operator** — not *sends*
- **versioned** / **recorded introduction receipt** — not *immutable*
- **potentially relevant** / **need one detail** / **not for us** / **suggest another professional** — these are the only four responses the concept offers

Why *immutable* is genuinely banned outright: it implies permanent storage, inability to correct, blockchain, ledger status, and evidence that cannot be withdrawn. The receipt must only establish *this context version was prepared for this stated purpose at this time* — correction and withdrawal stay possible.

Permission to display an operator's published catalogue is **not** a partnership. A link to a booking provider is **not** an integration.

## 5. How the cards get read afterwards

**Desk work, not stand work.** None of this appears on the card or in the conversation.

One headline number: **operator-to-operator handoffs per operator, last comparable season.**
Then weigh it against the other three signals recorded on the back of each card:

```
opportunity = frequency × booking value × context-loss cost × weakness of current workaround
```

| Frequency | Value | Friction | Read |
|---|---|---|---|
| High | Meaningful | High | Scenario B may be the business |
| Low | Very high | High | Premium feature for bespoke operators / DMCs |
| High | Low | Low | Probably not worth building |
| Low | Low | — | Scenario B dies |
| — | — | Workaround rated **4–5** | Do not build, regardless of story quality |
