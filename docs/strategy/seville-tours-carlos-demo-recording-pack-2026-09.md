## 0. Before you record

| Check | Why |
|---|---|
| One dev server only | Two servers on the same `.next` corrupts the build cache |
| Viewport 1280×800 | Verified free of horizontal overflow |
| `Reset all` before each take | Every demo starts from the same deterministic state |
| Do **not** click "Open gift card" | In dev it resolves to `localhost:3001`, which will look broken on camera |
| Hide the Next.js dev-tools badge | Bottom-right, not part of the product |
| Screen only, no webcam | Carlos is judging the work, not us |

**Claim discipline for every narration line.** Permitted: *nothing has been booked · availability has not been confirmed · nothing has been ordered · no price has been quoted · no redemption has occurred.* Never say: *booking confirmed · available now · order completed · voucher redeemed · price confirmed · request sent · introduction sent · immutable.*

The banner **Research concept · No real operator contacted** stays on screen for the entire recording. Do not crop it out.

## 4. Recording order and sending order

**Record in order 1 → 2 → 3.** Demo 1 is the hardest to pace, so do it while you are fresh; if you only get one clean take today, make it Demo 1.

**Recommended sending order — sequential:**

1. Send **Demo 1 only**, with Option A below.
2. If Carlos replies at all, send **Demo 2**.
3. Send **Demo 3** only once he has engaged with the immediate workflow, or has asked about longer-term value.

Rationale: Carlos is a solo operator in high season. Three videos in one message is a task; one video is a favour. The sequential order also tells us something the questionnaire cannot — **which demo he replies to first.**

Use the one-message option only if you have a scheduled call and need all three seen beforehand.

### Option C — Voice-note friendly

Text first:

> Hola Carlos. Te mando un vídeo de menos de dos minutos y una nota de voz explicando por qué. [enlace vídeo 1]

Then the voice note, around 20 seconds:

> "Hola Carlos, perdona la interrupción. Estoy preparando lo que vamos a enseñar en TIS y no quiero enseñar algo que a un operador de verdad no le sirva. El vídeo es corto. No necesito datos de tus clientes, y nada de lo que sale está reservado ni enviado. Lo que me interesa es si esto te quita trabajo o te lo añade. Si te parece inútil, dímelo tal cual. Gracias."

**Every option must say:** the video is short · the first questionnaire branch is short · honest criticism beats encouragement · no traveller data is needed · the purpose is deciding what to show at TIS · further conversation is optional.

## 6. What we watch for, beyond the answers

- **Which video he replies to first**, before any questionnaire response.
- **How long before he replies.** A same-day reply in October means something.
- **Whether he corrects a fact** about his own catalogue — that is the strongest possible engagement signal.
- **Whether he asks who else is using it.** That is a buying question, and it changes the TIS conversation.
